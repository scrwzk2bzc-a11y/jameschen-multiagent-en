The model sets the floor; constraints set the ceiling. Two weeks ago I thought the bottleneck was a model not smart enough; two weeks later the books on disk say the bottleneck is that every single "may I" had to pass one person. Roles change hands, sessions reopen, models get replaced — only the constraints on disk stay there, standing the gate for you. That is why every argument in this piece ultimately lands on files, fields, and mtime: they never change hands.

The criterion is not "did it finish," but "did it finish right, and how do you know." Anyone can answer the first half for you; only an institution can answer the second. Scoring weights, blocker gates, mtime forensics, filing lines — they are all parts of the second half; miss one and "knowing" loses a corner. Miss the forensics and all you have left is other people's narration; miss the filing and sign-off exemption turns into going dark.

What you are building is not an AI employee, but a process AI cannot run wild through. You are the process's first beneficiary: it trades your time from "watching every decision" to "designing how decisions get made." Dismantling "me" into institution is this system's true destination.
